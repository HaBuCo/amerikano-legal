Frequently asked questions

I can’t sign in

Check your internet connection and app version. If Google or Apple sign-in still fails, write to us and mention which sign-in method you use.

My online table is gone

Room codes and invitations can expire. Ask the host for a new invitation or create a new table.

Where is my guest account stored?

A guest session uses an anonymous account ID. Your profile, username, online games and friends data are kept on secure servers; single-player saves and preferences may be stored on your device.

I want my data deleted

In the app, use “Delete my account” at the bottom of Settings, or send a request from the account deletion page.
